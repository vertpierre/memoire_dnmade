Free/libre software & graphic design

This website was created as part of a final thesis in Graphic Design. It explores free/libre software and the ideas around it through the field of graphic design. I wanted to find out whether free software could help graphic designers rethink their practice and move towards a more ethical approach to design.
This text shows that, as designers, we do not always choose the practice we inherit. That can lead to forms of design that are not centred on the human. As Nolwenn Maudet explained in her thesis: The stagnation of the design tool landscape led to the progressive invisibility of design software in designers’ practice. Software kept out of sight in this way—including Adobe Creative Cloud—still shapes a specific practice even when we barely notice. Adobe retains full control over the proprietary tools it distributes and over the files they produce.
To avoid this loss of control over our tools and practice, I explored the philosophy of free software, centred on user freedom. I examined texts, projects and software, and interviewed users about their practice while questioning my own. Even though free software is often met with preconceptions, I concluded that it can give designers greater control over their tools and files while guiding them towards a more ethical practice.
